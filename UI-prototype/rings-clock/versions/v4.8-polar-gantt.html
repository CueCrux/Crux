What you're watching

The seam at 12 o'clock is "now". Sectors are ordered by age: the newest plan sits at 12:01, the oldest at 11:59 — position on the dial is seniority. When a new plan is born mid-playback it enters at the top and pushes the dial round; everything on the clock re-flows live.

Three exits, three flashes: teal seal = completed (its gates passed), grey = parked after 18 quiet days (the board's stale semantics — most of the 57 "in progress" plans are honestly this), red pulse = blocked.

Bars mode turns every node into a spoke from the heartwood to its ring — older spokes layer over newer, nodes spread clockwise in day order, so a plan reads as a fan of its own history. Click any bar or dot for its card. edge: inward flips the time metaphor: new work appears at the rim and sinks toward the heartwood as it ages.
