What this view can't tell you

  • ✕No signature to verify. The log is whatever the dashboard chooses to show — nothing is cryptographically signed, so you can't prove a row wasn't edited or dropped.
  • ✕No cross-agent handoff. One key, one flat stream. When work passes between agents or sessions, the chain of custody isn't recorded.
  • ✕No cost-per-agent attribution. Spend is aggregated per API key — you can't split it by which agent, tenant, or ExecPlan burned the tokens.
  • ✕Gone when you rotate the key. Rotate or revoke the key and the history tied to it disappears. There is no portable, self-held record.
