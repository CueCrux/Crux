## Methodology

Each dimension is scored 0–100 against a four-axis rubric. The rubric is deliberately blunt — partial-credit weighting is left to the operator on review.

| Axis | Max | Question |
|---|---|---|
| Free-tier shipped + verified | 30 | Are the Crux Daemon MCP tools + `corecruxctl` CLI both wired, tested, and passport-attributed? |
| Paid-tier shipped + verified | 30 | Are the `memory-core-mcp` / `vaultcrux-mcp` / Frontdoor surfaces wired and tested? |
| Cross-surface composition | 20 | Is the per-turn envelope wiring present, plus a console panel, plus host-IDE renderer hooks? |
| External parity | 20 | How does the dimension measure against the cited research benchmark (Mem0 2026, OpenTelemetry GenAI conventions, EU AI Act Art. 12/13/14/50, C2PA v2.3, NIST AI Agent Standards, METR)? |

**Scoring honesty.** This is an orchestrator's view. Where a milestone has visibly deferred sub-items (most often console SPA panels, interactive TUI menus, and host-IDE inline renderers in `crux-claude-hooks`), the relevant axis is docked. The scorecard measures **code-complete shipped behind a default-OFF flag**, not **live-by-default for end users**. The operator should adjust scores upward where the deferred surface is owned by a separate plan with a clear sequencing reason, or downward where the deferral is load-bearing for a user-facing claim.

**Evidence.** Every per-dimension score links the PRs that ship the work and the Crux fact ID that holds the `gate:M<n>` evidence with `commit_sha`, test counts, and feature-flag names. The fact IDs are stable; PR URLs may move if repos are renamed.

## Cited research benchmarks

- **Mem0 2026 state-of-memory** — readable/editable memory, freshness with explicit decay, narrative views, session continuity. We hit parity at the substrate level; gaps are in user-facing surfaces (narrative topic-browser, freshness panel, calm-deferred-output host renderer).
- **OpenTelemetry GenAI semantic conventions** — directly adopted on the spans emitted by #6's `tool_trace_recent`. Strongest external-parity score in the scorecard at 18/20.
- **EU AI Act Art. 12 / 13 / 14 / 50** — record-keeping (#11 + foundational receipts), transparency (#2, #4, #10), human oversight (#5), AI-generated content labelling (#7). Article 50 conformance achieved with the self-signed X.509 anchor; commercial-CA Adobe Verify green-tick deferred.
- **C2PA v2.3** — JUMBF-shaped manifest, P-256 signer, 90-day leaf rotation cadence. Offline verifier ships; public-viewer green-tick is the gap.
- **NIST AI Agent Standards Initiative** — identity continuity (#8), source-linked traceability (#4), visible autonomy (#10), typed action traces (#6). Strongest dimensions are #6 and #2; weakest is #10 (no end-user surface).
- **METR verification-debt study** — every minute the user spends checking the agent counts against the agent's value. #2 (acknowledged memory use) and #6 (typed traces) directly reduce verification debt; #4 (one-click verify) operationalises it.

## Caveats + known gaps

- **Wave 3 target missed.** Average 76.5/100 is below the ≥85 closeout target; #10 at 69 is below the ≥70 floor. Both miss-modes trace to the same deferral pattern: console SPA panels, interactive TUI menus, and host-IDE inline renderers in `crux-claude-hooks` are consistently deferred across plans because they require owner-led work in separate plans. The substrate (MCP tools + receipts + envelopes) is uniformly stronger than the user-facing surface.
- **Several Wave 2 PRs landed via admin-merge through a 2-week-running VaultCrux main CI regression** (`security:check:private-content` red since 2026-05-15 across 100+ main runs). Documented in fact `escalation:vaultcrux-main-regression-blocks-49`; diagnostic patch shipped in [VaultCrux PR #50](https://github.com/CueCrux/VaultCrux/pull/50) to log offending tenant_ids on the next failure (surfaces the 6th writer). Per the scorecard's honesty rule, the paid-tier axis on #4 is docked.
- **Adobe Verify public-viewer green-tick deferred** to a future commercial-CA SKU (~£500–1500/yr GlobalSign Atlas OV or DigiCert KeyLocker). Documented in fact `escalation:c2pa-trust-anchor-resolved`. Offline verification via the distributed self-signed anchor is fully functional today and satisfies EU AI Act Art. 50 transparency intent.
- **All new tools are flagged default-OFF** per master-plan §Constraints "CI-gated". The scorecard measures **code-complete shipped**, not **live-by-default-for-end-users**. Operator-side flag flip + per-tenant `__tenant_metadata__::<tenant>` allowlist drive rollout.
- **Receipt-clean rollback preserved.** If any flag is rolled back, receipts emitted while it was on remain valid and replayable — no schema migrations break old receipts.
